FRAIMOUS TV App Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

FRAIMOUS ("we", "us") provides the FRAIMOUS art app for TVs and streaming devices. This policy explains what the app collects.

Information We Collect

  • Device Pairing Token. When you unlock content, the app creates a random token stored on your TV. It is used only to remember what you have unlocked. It is not linked to your name.
  • Pairing Codes. These are short-lived 6-character codes that connect your TV to the purchase page on your phone.
  • Purchase Records. If you buy a pack or membership, our payment processor (for example Stripe) handles your payment details. We receive confirmation of the purchase and, where provided, your email for receipts. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Basic Technical Logs. Our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP address, time, device type) for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

How We Use Information

To deliver and remember unlocked artwork, to process purchases, to prevent fraud and to keep the service running.

Retention

Pairing codes expire. Unlock records are kept while your purchase or membership is active, and as required for accounting.

Your Choices

You can clear the app's data on your device at any time, which removes the stored token. To request deletion of purchase records, contact us at hello@fraimous.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will post updates to this page.
